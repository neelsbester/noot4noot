Design principles

Less interface. More game.

Quiet hierarchy

Editorial type and generous spacing replace oversized labels, glow, and constant borders.

Purposeful colour

Muted decade colours identify cards; green, rust, and ochre communicate actions and state.

Phone first

Primary actions remain thumb-sized and obvious without turning every control into a loud button.

Calm motion

Animation explains card movement and results, rather than decorating idle screens.

Core palette

Warm neutral surfaces, dark ink, and subdued accents. Existing decade mapping remains, but saturation is reduced so cards feel collected rather than fluorescent.
